# Email Deliverability

UpDrafted sends transactional email from `support@updrafted.us` with a
`Reply-To: support@updrafted.us` header. Keep that mailbox monitored so users
and mailbox providers can treat these messages as replyable.

## Required DNS records

Publish these records in the DNS provider for `updrafted.us`.

### DMARC

Host:

```text
_dmarc
```

TXT value:

```text
v=DMARC1; p=none; rua=mailto:dmarc@updrafted.us; adkim=s; aspf=s
```

Start with `p=none` so reports can be monitored without blocking mail. After
SPF and DKIM alignment look healthy, move to `p=quarantine`, then `p=reject`.

### SPF and DKIM

DMARC depends on SPF or DKIM passing and aligning with `updrafted.us`. Keep the
SPF and DKIM records generated by the active email provider, currently Resend,
published for the sending domain.

## Provider checklist

- Verify `updrafted.us` in Resend.
- Confirm Resend DKIM records are present and passing.
- Confirm the SPF record includes Resend's sending mechanism.
- Create or route `support@updrafted.us`.
- Create or route `dmarc@updrafted.us` if aggregate reports are enabled.
